FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. HOW DOES ABC PROTECT YOUR DATA?

Your websites and applications are hosted on Google Cloud. Google invests heavily in securing its infrastructure to protect your data throughout its lifecycle. This infrastructure is secured in multiple layers using advanced technology.

2. WHAT ARE ABC'S CUSTOMER DATA POLICIES?

You retain all rights to your data. We make it easy to export your data and move it elsewhere if you choose.

Your contact information is never shared or sold to third parties, except for the limited purposes described in our Privacy Policy.

While your account or contract is active, you can access, view, or transfer your information at any time. Information can be downloaded in several common formats.

3. HOW DOES ABC MAINTAIN SERVICE CONTINUITY AND RESPOND TO UNEXPECTED INCIDENTS?

Our cloud data systems use Google Cloud, which is designed with multiple layers of physical security. Google also operates servers in third-party data centers that maintain physical safeguards against unexpected incidents.

Technical support is provided at no additional charge while you use ABC services.
